# Privacy Policy — CricZen

**Effective date:** October 1, 2026

CricZen is an open-source, ad-free cricket live scores app. This policy explains
what the app does with your information — in short: **we collect nothing**.

## Data we collect

None. CricZen has:

- **No accounts** — you never sign in.
- **No analytics, no trackers, no ads.**
- **No data sent to us** — there is no CricZen server.

## Data stored on your device

The following stays on your phone and never leaves it:

- Your favourite-team preferences and notification settings.
- Cached match scores and news, so the app loads faster.

You can clear this at any time via Android Settings → Apps → CricZen → Storage.

## Network use

To show live scores and news, the app fetches public data from third-party
cricket data providers over the internet. Tapping a full scorecard opens
Cricbuzz in an in-app browser tab — Cricbuzz's own privacy policy applies there.

## Notifications

If you enable match alerts, they are scheduled locally on your device.

## Permissions

CricZen requests only what it needs: internet access (scores/news) and the
ability to post notifications and run the home-screen widget.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app
release on GitHub.
