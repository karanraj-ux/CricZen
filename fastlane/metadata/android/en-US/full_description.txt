Find your focus. Follow your game.

CricZen is an open-source, ad-free cricket companion for Android. No clutter, no betting ads — just your teams, your players, your scores, gentle on battery and data.

• Live scores dashboard with auto-refresh, offline caching, pinned matches, target predictions and run-chase progress bars
• News that finds you: a personalized Top Stories rail, news woven into the match feed, and related news on every match page
• Smart notifications for wickets, milestones and match events — league picks expand to franchises automatically
• Fan Mode: set your idol's wallpaper as the app header, with a live match-day header, Fan Favorites, and Zenny (CricBot), an offline cricket historian
• Home-screen widget, picture-in-picture pop-out score, dark mode and Data Saver mode
• Full Cricbuzz scorecards open right inside the app

100% free and open source (MIT). No accounts, no tracking, no ads.
